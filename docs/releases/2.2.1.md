Version 2.2.1 adds a time program editor to the Heat pump view and makes the controller's energy counters usable in Home Assistant's Energy dashboard.

- **Time program:** set when each heating circuit runs at its normal setpoint and when at its night setpoint. Each circuit has a week view where you drag or type the times. You can copy a week to other circuits or remove the schedule. Nothing is written to the heat pump until you press **Apply**, and only the switch points that changed are sent.
- **Clearer circuit tiles:** a circuit only shows the setpoints its mode uses. Comfort with a night setback shows both, plus a bar with today's program. Comfort without one offers to set up a time program.
- **Live electrical power:** the compressor's current electrical power appears next to the heat output.
- **Separate cards:** the heating curve and the 24-hour energy statistics are now two cards (`ochsner-curve-card`, `ochsner-energy-card`), and the time program has its own card (`ochsner-schedule-card`). The combined heating curve & efficiency card keeps working.
- **Energy dashboard:** the controller's energy counters are now energy sensors. The current-year and lifetime counters keep long-term statistics, so you can add them under **Settings → Dashboards → Energy → Individual devices**, for example *Total electrical energy consumption, current year*. Setups that were missing the yearly totals get them added.

Update **Ochsner Local OTS** to **v2.2.1** in HACS, then restart Home Assistant. Existing entities, devices and their history are kept.
