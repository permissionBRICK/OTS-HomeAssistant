Version 2.3.0 backs up the heat pump's settings and restores them (issue #35).

- **Settings backups:** the integration keeps backups of the settings you can change on the heat pump: heating and cooling curves, heating limits, building inertia, room and hot water setpoints, operating programs, circuit names, sensor offsets and the time programs. It takes one automatically a minute after Home Assistant starts, whenever something changed since the last one. **Back up settings** on the device page takes one on demand, and the **Last settings backup** sensor shows when the newest was taken. A backup only reads the controller and costs no flash writes. Home Assistant's own backups include them.
- **Restore:** under **Configure**, tick **Restore heat pump settings from a backup**. The restore lists every setting that differs from the backup, with its current and backed-up value. Untick what should stay. Only the differences are written, and the current settings are backed up first, so you can go back.
- **Backup file:** **Download diagnostics** on the device page saves the backups to a file, which the restore accepts again. The file contains no password, PIN, network address or serial number.
- **No more deprecation warnings** on Home Assistant 2026.11 (the changes of 2.2.3).

Update **Ochsner Local OTS** to **v2.3.0** in HACS, then restart Home Assistant. Existing entities, devices and their history are kept. Two entities are new on the heat pump's device: **Back up settings** and **Last settings backup**.
