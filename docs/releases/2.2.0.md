Version 2.2.0 adds a heat pump dashboard, Home Assistant thermostats for each heating circuit and a water heater for hot water.

**Heat pump view in the sidebar.** A new **Heat pump** page shows a schematic of your plant with live temperatures, the controls you would otherwise use in the Ochsner app, the heating curve of each circuit and the last 24 hours of heat output split into electricity and ambient heat. The page builds itself from what your controller reports, the same way the Ochsner app decides what to show: your circuits with their names, cooling only for circuits that can cool, and hot water, buffer tank, auxiliary heater and pool only if they exist.

**Room setpoints.** The controls change a circuit's room setpoints directly. The big −/+ is the setpoint of the selected mode: the normal (day) setpoint in Komfort, the reduced setpoint in Eco and the old "Reduziert" program. The other one sits in a small row below it, labelled Night or Day, so the night setback in Komfort can be changed too. Circuits that can cool show their cooling setpoints while they cool. The setpoint the circuit currently follows is marked. The Ochsner app's temporary ±3 K correction no longer has its own stepper on the card; while it is set, a chip shows it and resets it to 0. The thermostat entity keeps the app's correction behaviour. Circuits with a room sensor show the room temperature and humidity in the schematic and the controls.

**Heating curve editing.** Drag the curve points, the heating limit or the maximum flow temperature, or use the arrow keys on a focused point. The chart shows what the change means at today's outdoor temperature. Nothing is written to the heat pump until you press **Apply**.

**Cards.** The same three parts are available as cards in the dashboard card picker (search for "Ochsner"): plant schematic, controls, and heating curve with efficiency, plus one card that combines all three.

**Thermostats and water heater.** Each heating circuit gets a `climate` entity, so Home Assistant's thermostat and tile cards, voice assistants and HomeKit work with it. On/off, Komfort/Eco, heat/cool/auto (for circuits that can cool) and the room setpoint correction behave like the corresponding buttons in the Ochsner app. Plants with hot water also get a `water_heater` entity and a **Hot water boost** button. Each circuit additionally gets a **Komfort/Eco** select and a **Room setpoint correction** number.

**Fewer writes to the controller.** The controller stores settings in flash memory with a limited number of write cycles. Rapid −/+ taps are sent as one write once you stop tapping, values that didn't change aren't written (every command reads the current values from the heat pump first), the heating curve is written only on **Apply**, and switching the heat pump off asks for confirmation.

**New installations** ask once whether to show the heat pump view in the sidebar. **Existing installations** get the sidebar view switched on after the update, together with a notification. To hide it, open **Settings → Devices & services → Ochsner Local OTS → Configure** and switch off **Show the heat pump view in the sidebar**. The same page lets you choose between your Home Assistant theme's colours (default) and the Ochsner app's colours.

Update **Ochsner Local OTS** to **v2.2.0** in HACS, then fully restart Home Assistant. Existing entities, devices and their history are kept unchanged. This version requires Home Assistant 2026.8 or newer.

Validated on Home Assistant 2026.10.2:
- **269 tests passed**, with **21 skipped** because historical APK/bundle research files were unavailable, and **106 frontend tests passed**, including browser tests for dragging the heating curve with a mouse, by touch and with the keyboard.
- End-to-end tests in a real Home Assistant against a simulated controller built from an AirHawk 518 snapshot: new installation with and without the sidebar view, update from 2.1.3 (all existing entity IDs unchanged), switching the sidebar view off and on, heating curve dragging with Apply/Discard/keyboard, −/+ coalescing, Komfort/Eco, heat pump off confirmation, the cards on a dashboard and US customary units.
- Live on an AirHawk 518: the update kept all 441 existing entities and added the sidebar view and the notification. Changing the room setpoint correction, Komfort/Eco and the heating curve from the view reached the heat pump, and every value was set back afterwards. That test showed that the controller recalculates its targets a few seconds after a change, so a correction made right after another change is now sent once the controller has settled.

Hot water, cooling circuits, room sensors and pools follow the Ochsner app's code but couldn't be tested on real hardware; the test plant has three heating-only circuits and no hot water tank. Please open an issue if something looks wrong on your plant.
