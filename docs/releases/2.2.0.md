Version 2.2.0 adds a heat pump dashboard to Home Assistant, plus a thermostat for each heating circuit and a water heater for hot water.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/permissionBRICK/OTS-HomeAssistant/v2.2.0/docs/images/mobile-heating-dark.png" alt="Heat pump view on a phone while heating" width="320">

- **Heat pump view in the sidebar:** a live schematic of your plant, the controls from the Ochsner app, the heating curve of each circuit and the last 24 hours of heat output. The page builds itself from what your controller reports, so it only shows the circuits, hot water tank, buffer, auxiliary heater and pool your plant has.
- **Room setpoints:** change each circuit's day, night and reduced setpoints directly, and switch between Comfort, Eco and Off.
- **Heating curve editing:** drag the curve points, the heating limit or the maximum flow temperature. Nothing is written to the heat pump until you press **Apply**.
- **Cards:** the schematic, the controls and the heating curve are also available as dashboard cards. Search for "Ochsner" in the card picker.
- **Thermostats and water heater:** each heating circuit gets a `climate` entity, so thermostat cards, voice assistants and HomeKit work with it. Plants with hot water get a `water_heater` entity and a **Hot water boost** button.
- **Fewer writes to the controller:** rapid −/+ taps are sent as one write, unchanged values aren't written, and switching the heat pump off asks for confirmation.

Existing installations get the sidebar view switched on after the update, together with a notification. To hide it, open **Settings → Devices & services → Ochsner Local OTS → Configure**. The same page lets you switch to the Ochsner app's colours.

Update **Ochsner Local OTS** to **v2.2.0** in HACS, then fully restart Home Assistant. Existing entities, devices and their history are kept. This version requires Home Assistant 2026.8 or newer.

Tested on Home Assistant 2026.10.2 (269 tests and 135 frontend tests passed), end to end against a simulated controller, and live on an AirHawk 518 with three heating circuits. Hot water, cooling circuits, room sensors and pools follow the Ochsner app's code but couldn't be tested on real hardware. Please open an issue if something looks wrong on your plant. All details are in [PR #26](https://github.com/permissionBRICK/OTS-HomeAssistant/pull/26).
