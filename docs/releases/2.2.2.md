Version 2.2.2 tidies up the circuit tiles in the Heat pump view.

- **No "Set up time program" link on the tiles:** a circuit in Comfort without a night setback now shows just its normal setpoint. You set up a night setback on the Time program card, which is on the same view. Circuits that already have a night setback keep both setpoints, today's bar and the link to their program.
- **Flow temperatures on wide tiles:** with the link gone, the flow temperatures move into its place on the right, as they already did in Eco and Reduced.

Update **Ochsner Local OTS** to **v2.2.2** in HACS, then restart Home Assistant. Existing entities, devices and their history are kept.
