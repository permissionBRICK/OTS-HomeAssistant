Version 2.2.2 leaves the Time program card out of the Heat pump view until you need it.

- **Time program on request:** while no heating circuit has a night setback, the Heat pump view no longer shows the Time program card. Click **Set up time program** on a circuit tile to show it. It stays while you set up the program, and its **✕** hides it again if you decide against one. Once a night setback is saved, the card stays. The separate `ochsner-schedule-card` always shows.
- **Scrolling to the time program:** the link on a circuit tile now scrolls the card fully into view, below the toolbar.

Update **Ochsner Local OTS** to **v2.2.2** in HACS, then restart Home Assistant. Existing entities, devices and their history are kept.
